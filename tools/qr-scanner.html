Frequently asked questions

How do I scan a QR code?

Upload an image of a QR code using the scanner on the main page; Qrzo decodes it in your browser.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The image is read locally and decoded on your device; it never leaves your browser.

What can it decode?

Any standard QR code — URLs, text, WiFi, contacts, email, SMS, and calendar events.
