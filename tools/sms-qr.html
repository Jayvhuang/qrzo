Frequently asked questions

Does it pre-fill the message?

Yes. The recipient number and the message body are both encoded, so the user only taps send.

Will it cost the scanner anything?

The scanner pays their normal carrier SMS rate; Qrzo does not charge anything.

Is it processed locally?

Yes. The SMS link is built in your browser and never uploaded.
