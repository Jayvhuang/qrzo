Frequently asked questions

Is generating a URL QR code free?

Yes. Qrzo generates URL QR codes entirely in your browser and is completely free, with no watermark.

Do the QR codes expire?

No. Qrzo creates static QR codes, so the link is baked into the image and works as long as the destination is live.

Is my link uploaded to a server?

Never. The URL is encoded locally in your browser and is never sent to any server.
