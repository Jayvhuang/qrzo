Frequently asked questions

What kind of text can I encode?

Any plain text — a greeting, a reminder, a coupon code, or a short instruction.

Is there a length limit?

Longer text produces a denser QR code. Keep it reasonably short for the most reliable scanning.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Both generation and scanning happen on your device; no internet is required.
