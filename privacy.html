Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-23

1. Your content never leaves your device

Qrzo generates and decodes QR codes entirely inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript. The text, links, contact details, and images you use are read and processed on your own device and are never uploaded to, transmitted to, or stored on any server operated by Qrzo. When you close the tab, the in-memory data is discarded.

2. Third-party cookies, web beacons, and IP addresses

This website may display advertising provided by Google AdSense. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons (also called pixel tags or clear GIFs), and may collect your IP address and other technical information such as browser type and referring pages. These technologies are set and controlled by Google, not by Qrzo. Qrzo does not sell or share your personal information.

3. Google's use of data

Google's use of advertising cookies and web beacons enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your prior visits. You can review how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites and apps here:

https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/partners/

You can manage or disable personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings and via industry opt-out pages such as YourAdChoices.

4. Amazon Associates disclosure

Qrzo is a participant in the Amazon Associates program, an affiliate advertising program. As an Amazon Associate, Qrzo earns from qualifying purchases. Some links may be affiliate links; this does not affect the price you pay.

5. Children's privacy

Qrzo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us via the information on the website footer. Because content is processed locally, we generally do not have access to the data you process.
